# Complete Speed Calculation Mastery Curriculum (Arun Sharma Method)

A level-based, untimed mental calculation curriculum covering all four chapters of Arun Sharma's *Quantitative Aptitude* ("First Things First: Calculation Techniques").

Designed for **SSC CGL, CAT, and Banking** aspirants to eliminate pencil-and-paper reliance, build instinctual reflexes, and save 30+ minutes in competitive exams.

---

## 1. Core Design Philosophy: Mastery Over Panic

* **Zero Countdown Stress:** Standard timer pressure causes guessing and reinforces bad habits. All levels are **untimed by default**.
* **Unlock Threshold:** To unlock the next level, achieve **3 consecutive rounds at 100% mental accuracy**.
* **Alternating 5 & 10 Node Rhythm:**
  * **5-Node Levels ("Sprint / Reflex"):** Internalize decomposition techniques.
  * **10-Node Levels ("Stamina / Endurance"):** Working-memory load and consecutive century crossings.
* **Ghost Hop Thought Process:** An interactive thought visualizer reveals the exact mental jump for any problem.

## 4. Module 3: Multiplications (Chapter 2)

Covers all 5 distinct multiplication techniques detailed in the book:

### Track A: Vedic / Trachtenberg Criss-Cross (Straight-Line Method)
* **Lvl 1 (2-Digit Base):** Units cross, tens cross ($43 \times 78$):
  * Units: $3 \times 8 = 24 \to$ write $4$, carry $2$.
  * Cross: $(4 \times 8) + (3 \times 7) + 2 = 32 + 21 + 2 = 55 \to$ write $5$, carry $5$.
  * Tens: $(4 \times 7) + 5 = 33 \to \mathbf{3354}$.
* **Lvl 2 (2-Digit $\times$ 3-Digit):** e.g., $324 \times 82$.
* **Lvl 3 (Full 3-Digit $\times$ 3-Digit):** Single-line mental expansion.

### Track B: Difference of Squares $(a^2 - b^2)$
Applies when two numbers are symmetric around a round anchor:
* **Lvl 1 (Even Gaps):** 
  * $18 \times 22 = 20^2 - 2^2 = 400 - 4 = \mathbf{396}$
  * $22 \times 28 = 25^2 - 3^2 = 625 - 9 = \mathbf{616}$
  * $35 \times 47 = 41^2 - 6^2 = 1681 - 36 = \mathbf{1645}$
* **Lvl 2 (Odd Gap Adjustments):**
  * $24 \times 33 = (24 \times 32) + 24 = (28^2 - 4^2) + 24 = 768 + 24 = \mathbf{792}$.

### Track C: Base Deviation Method (Close to 100 & 1000)
* **Lvl 1 (Both Below 100):** $94 \times 96 \to$ Deviations $(-6, -4)$:
  * Initial digits: $94 + (-4) = 90$
  * Last 2 digits: $(-6) \times (-4) = 24 \implies \mathbf{9024}$.
* **Lvl 2 (Both Above 100):** $102 \times 103 \to (+2, +3) \implies 105 \mid 06 = \mathbf{10506}$.
* **Lvl 3 (With Carry):** $84 \times 88 \to (-16, -12) \implies (-16 \times -12 = 192 \to 92 \text{ and carry } 1) \implies \mathbf{7392}$.
* **Lvl 4 (One Above, One Below):** $104 \times 92 \to (+4, -8) \implies 96 \to 9600 - 32 = \mathbf{9568}$.
* **Lvl 5 (Base 1000):** $994 \times 996 \to (-6, -4) \implies \mathbf{990024}$.

### Track D: Percentage Decomposition Method
Using percentages to multiply rapidly for Data Interpretation:
* **Lvl 1:** $43 \times 78 \implies 43\% \text{ of } 78 \times 100$:
  * $10\% \text{ of } 78 = 7.8 \times 4 = 31.2$
  * $1\% \text{ of } 78 = 0.78 \times 3 = 2.34$
  * Total $= 33.54 \times 100 = \mathbf{3354}$.
* **Lvl 2:** High-speed DI estimation with decimals.

## 5. Module 4: Divisions, Percentages & Ratio Comparisons (Chapter 3)

### Track A: Decimal Values via Percentage Additions
Converts difficult fraction division into additive percentages:
* **Lvl 1 (Single 10% Step):** $53 / 81$:
  * Denominator $= 81$.
  * $50\% = 40.5$, Remainder $= 12.5$.
  * $10\% = 8.1$, Remainder $= 4.4 \implies$ Answer is in $60\%-70\%$ range ($0.6\dots$).
* **Lvl 2 (Fine Tuning to 1% & 0.1%):**
  * $1\% = 0.81 \implies 5\% = 4.05$, Remainder $= 0.35$.
  * $0.1\% = 0.081 \implies 4 \times 0.081 = 0.324$.
  * Value $= 65.43\% \implies \mathbf{0.6543}$.
* **Lvl 3 (Improper Fractions):** $223 / 72 \implies 300\% + 9\% + 0.7\% \implies \mathbf{3.097}$.

### Track B: Ratio Comparison Face-Off
Comparing fractions without long division ($A/B$ vs $C/D$):
* **Lvl 1 (Visual Logic):** If Numerator increases and Denominator decreases $\to$ instant higher ratio.
* **Lvl 2 (Cross Multiplication Sprint):** Compare $12/17$ vs $15/19 \implies 12 \times 19$ vs $15 \times 17$.
* **Lvl 3 (10% Bracket Positioning):**
  * Compare $173/212$ with $181/241$.
  * $173/212 > 80\%$, while $181/241 < 80\% \implies$ First ratio is immediately larger.
* **Lvl 4 (Numerator-Denominator % Change Method):**
  * Compare $173/212$ with $181/225$.
  * Numerator increases by $+4.6\%$, Denominator increases by $+6.1\%$.
  * Since Denominator grew faster $\implies 173/212 > 181/225$.

## 6. Module 5: Squares & Cubes (Chapter 4)

### Track A: Squares Shortcuts
* **Lvl 1 (The Foundation Table):** 1 to 30 Squares reflex.
* **Lvl 2 (Ending in 5):** $N5^2 = N(N+1) \mid 25$ (e.g., $85^2 = 8 \times 9 \mid 25 = \mathbf{7225}$; $135^2 = 13 \times 14 \mid 25 = \mathbf{18225}$).
* **Lvl 3 (Base 50: 51 to 80):**
  * Rule: $(25 + x) \mid x^2$ where $x = N - 50$.
  * Example: $67^2 \to x = 17 \implies (25 + 17) \mid 17^2 = 42 \mid 289 \implies \mathbf{4489}$.
  * Example: $76^2 \to x = 26 \implies (25 + 26) \mid 26^2 = 51 \mid 676 \implies \mathbf{5776}$.
* **Lvl 4 (Base 50: 31 to 50):**
  * Rule: $(25 - x) \mid x^2$ where $x = 50 - N$.
  * Example: $41^2 \to x = 9 \implies (25 - 9) \mid 9^2 = 16 \mid 81 = \mathbf{1681}$.
  * Example: $36^2 \to x = 14 \implies (25 - 14) \mid 14^2 = 11 \mid 196 \implies \mathbf{1296}$.
* **Lvl 5 (Base 100: 81 to 100):**
  * Rule: $(N - x) \mid x^2$ where $x = 100 - N$.
  * Example: $82^2 \to x = 18 \implies (82 - 18) \mid 18^2 = 64 \mid 324 \implies \mathbf{6724}$.
  * Example: $87^2 \to x = 13 \implies (87 - 13) \mid 13^2 = 74 \mid 169 \implies \mathbf{7569}$.

### Track B: 2-Digit Cubes (Geometric Progression Method)
* **Lvl 1 (GP Sequence Method):**
  * For $28^3$: Ratio of unit to tens digit $= 8 / 2 = 4$.
  * First term $= 2^3 = 8$. Next terms multiply by 4: $[8,\ 32,\ 128,\ 512]$.
  * Double middle two terms: $[+64,\ +256]$.
  * Add columns with carry:
    * $512 \to$ write $2$, carry $51$.
    * $128 + 256 + 51 = 435 \to$ write $5$, carry $43$.
    * $32 + 64 + 43 = 139 \to$ write $9$, carry $13$.
    * $8 + 13 = 21 \implies \mathbf{21952}$.
* **Lvl 2 (Cube Root Extraction):** Reverse estimation by checking last digit and cube boundaries.

## 8. Summary of Better Approaches & Brainstorming Points

1. **Integrated Diagnostic Radar:** The app tracks which technique is your weakest (e.g., strong at $50-70$ additions, but slow on Base 50 squares) and auto-recommends your daily 3-level workout.
2. **Interactive Scratchpad-Free UI:** Big, clean numbers with clear visual focal points so your brain doesn't squint or feel overwhelmed.
3. **Step-by-Step Training vs. Rapid-Fire Mode:** Every drill has two tabs:
   * **Learn / Step-by-Step:** The app asks for the intermediate components (e.g., in Base 100: "What are the two deviations?", "What is the cross sum?").
   * **Instinct / One-Shot:** You only enter the final answer after doing all steps mentally.
