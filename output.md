**Q1.** Suresh can save 20% of his income. But after 4 years, his income increases by 40% and he still manages to save the same amount as before. What is the percent increase in his expenditure?
- (a) 50 (b) 22 (c) 45 (d) 23

**Solution:**
Let income = I. Savings = 20% of I = 0.2I. Expenditure = I - 0.2I = 0.8I.
After 4 years: Income = I + 40% of I = 1.4I. Savings = 0.2I (same).
New expenditure = 1.4I - 0.2I = 1.2I.
Increase in expenditure = 1.2I - 0.8I = 0.4I.
% increase = (0.4I / 0.8I) × 100 = 50%.
- **Answer: (a) 50**

**Q2.** 35% of the students in a college are girls and the rest are boys. The total number of students in the college is 2800. 75% of the boys and 85% of the girls passed the final examination. The percentage of total students who passed the final examination is ?
- (a) 80% (b) 82% (c) 78% (d) 78.5%

**Solution:**
Total students = 2800.
Girls = 35% of 2800 = 980. Boys = 65% of 2800 = 1820.
Girls passed = 85% of 980 = 833. Boys passed = 75% of 1820 = 1365.
Total passed = 833 + 1365 = 2198.
% passed = (2198 / 2800) × 100 = 78.5%.
- **Answer: (d) 78.5%**

**Q3.** In 2003, the total population of a village was 4800, out of which 40% were females. In 2004, the total population increased by 10% as compared to the previous year. If the number of females remained the same in 2004, what was the percentage increase in the number of males in 2004 as compared to 2003 ?
- (1) 18% (2) 20% (3) 15% (4) 16(2/3)%

**Solution:**
2003: Total = 4800. Females = 40% = 1920. Males = 60% = 2880.
2004: Total = 4800 × 1.1 = 5280. Females = 1920 (same). Males = 5280 - 1920 = 3360.
Increase in males = 3360 - 2880 = 480.
% increase = (480 / 2880) × 100 = 16.666...% = 16(2/3)%.
- **Answer: (4) 16(2/3)%**

**Q4.** In a city 39% of the men are married and 51% of the women are married. Considering that nobody is married more than once. What percent of population is married?
- A. 44% B. 44.2% C. 43% D. 44.50%

**Solution:**
Let men = m, women = w.
Married men = 0.39m, Married women = 0.51w.
Since each marriage pairs 1 man + 1 woman: 0.39m = 0.51w.
So m/w = 0.51/0.39 = 51/39 = 17/13.
Let m = 17k, w = 13k. Total population = 30k.
Married people = 0.39 × 17k + 0.51 × 13k = 6.63k + 6.63k = 13.26k.
% married = (13.26k / 30k) × 100 = 44.2%.
- **Answer: B. 44.2%**

**Q5.** In a city 45% of the men are married and 25% of women are married. Considering that nobody is married more than once. What percent of population is married?
- 35%, 35(1/7)%, 32(1/7)%, 34%

**Solution:**
Married men = 0.45m, Married women = 0.25w.
0.45m = 0.25w ⇒ m/w = 25/45 = 5/9.
Let m = 5k, w = 9k. Total = 14k.
Married = 0.45 × 5k + 0.25 × 9k = 2.25k + 2.25k = 4.5k.
% married = (4.5k / 14k) × 100 = (9/28) × 100 = 32.1428...% = 32(1/7)%.
- **Answer: 32(1/7)%**

**Q6.** Rita spends 25% of her monthly income on house rent and 30% of the remaining income on food. If she saves ₹5250, what is her monthly income?
- (a) ₹12000 (b) ₹16000 (c) ₹10000 (d) ₹14000

**Solution:**
Let income = I.
Rent = 25% of I = 0.25I. Remaining = I - 0.25I = 0.75I.
Food = 30% of remaining = 0.3 × 0.75I = 0.225I.
Total spent = 0.25I + 0.225I = 0.475I.
Savings = I - 0.475I = 0.525I = 5250.
I = 5250 / 0.525 = 10000.
- **Answer: (c) ₹10000**

**Q7.** Rajan spent 10% of his salary on rent. He spent 20% of the remaining part of the salary on transport. After which he spent 40% of the balance of the salary on food. Further, he spent 80% of the balance on various bills. He deposits ₹5000 in the bank and kept the remaining ₹1480 for his own petty expenditure. Find his monthly salary (in ₹).
- (a) 75000 (b) 80000 (c) 64800 (d) 82500

**Solution:**
Let salary = S.
Rent = 0.1S. Remaining = 0.9S.
Transport = 0.2 × 0.9S = 0.18S. Remaining = 0.9S - 0.18S = 0.72S.
Food = 0.4 × 0.72S = 0.288S. Remaining = 0.72S - 0.288S = 0.432S.
Bills = 0.8 × 0.432S = 0.3456S. Remaining = 0.432S - 0.3456S = 0.0864S.
This remaining = bank + petty = 5000 + 1480 = 6480.
So 0.0864S = 6480 ⇒ S = 6480 / 0.0864 = 75000.
- **Answer: (a) 75000**

**Q8.** A man started off a business with a certain capital amount. In the first year, he earned 60% profit and donated 50% of the total capital (initial amount+profit). He followed the same procedure with the remaining capital after the second and the third year. If at the end of the three years, he is left with 15,360, what was the initial amount with which the man started his business?
- 1. 20,000 2. 30,000 3. 25,000 4. 32,000

**Solution:**
Let initial = x.
Each year: Total = Remaining × 1.6 (60% profit), then donate 50%, so remaining = Total × 0.5.
So each year: Remaining = (Previous × 1.6) × 0.5 = Previous × 0.8.
After 3 years: x × (0.8)³ = 15360.
x × 0.512 = 15360 ⇒ x = 15360 / 0.512 = 30000.
- **Answer: 2. 30,000**

**Q9.** A man started off a business with a certain capital amount. In the first year, he earned 33.33% profit and donated 20% of the total capital (initial amount+profit). He followed the same procedure with the remaining capital after the second and the third year. If at the end of the three years, he is left with 4096, what was the initial amount with which the man started his business?
- 1. 2785 2. 3325 3. 3550 4. 3375

**Solution:**
33.33% = 1/3.
Each year: Total = Remaining × (4/3). Donate 20%, so remaining = Total × 0.8 = (4/3) × 0.8 = 4/3 × 4/5 = 16/15 of previous.
After 3 years: x × (16/15)³ = 4096.
x × (4096 / 3375) = 4096 ⇒ x = 3375.
- **Answer: 4. 3375**

**Q10.** Gita purchase an apartment and pays EMI for that. Her monthly income is ₹80,000. She spends 15% on the education of her children 40% on household affairs, sends 10% to GPF, saves 15% in a savings bank account and pays the rest of the amount as EMI. How much EMI does she pay ?
- (a) ₹16,000 (b) ₹14,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹20,000

**Solution:**
Income = 80000.
Education = 15% = 12000. Household = 40% = 32000. GPF = 10% = 8000. Savings = 15% = 12000.
Total spent (excluding EMI) = 12000 + 32000 + 8000 + 12000 = 64000.
EMI = 80000 - 64000 = 16000.
- **Answer: (a) ₹16,000**

**Q11.** Sujata spends 18% of her monthly income on house rent, 40% of the income on groceries and 55% of the remaining on her children education and others. If her only savings is Rs. 4,725 then her expenditure on 'education and others' is:
- (a) Rs. 5,885 (b) Rs. 5,755 (c) Rs. 5,875 (d) Rs. 5,775

**Solution:**
Let income = I.
Rent = 0.18I. Groceries = 0.4I. Total so far = 0.58I. Remaining = 0.42I.
Education & others = 55% of remaining = 0.55 × 0.42I = 0.231I.
Total expenses = 0.18I + 0.4I + 0.231I = 0.811I.
Savings = I - 0.811I = 0.189I = 4725.
I = 4725 / 0.189 = 25000.
Education expenditure = 0.231 × 25000 = 5775.
- **Answer: (d) Rs. 5,775**

**Q12.** Out of total income a person's expenditure on food, cloth and other items are in the ratio of 4 : 5 : 3 respectively. If his expenditure is increased by 10% on food and 20% on cloth and decreased by 5% on other items. What percent should his income be increased so that no effect is recorded on the consumption of food, cloth and other items ?
- (a) 6.25% (c) 10.5% (b) 10.41% (d) 12.5%

**Solution:**
Let expenditure = 4k + 5k + 3k = 12k.
New: Food = 4k × 1.1 = 4.4k. Cloth = 5k × 1.2 = 6k. Other = 3k × 0.95 = 2.85k.
New total = 4.4k + 6k + 2.85k = 13.25k.
Increase = (13.25k - 12k) / 12k = 1.25/12 = 0.104166... = 10.42% ≈ 10.41%.
- **Answer: (b) 10.41%**

**Q13.** A, B and C spend 80%, 85% and 75% of their incomes, respectively. If their savings are in the ratio 8 : 9 : 20 and the difference between the incomes of A and C is ₹18,000, then the income of B is :
- (a) ₹24,000 (b) ₹36,000 (c) ₹27,000 (d) ₹30,000

**Solution:**
A saves 20%, B saves 15%, C saves 25%.
Savings ratio A:B:C = 8:9:20. Let savings be 8k, 9k, 20k.
Income of A = 8k / 0.2 = 40k.
Income of B = 9k / 0.15 = 60k.
Income of C = 20k / 0.25 = 80k.
Difference (C - A) = 80k - 40k = 40k = 18000 ⇒ k = 450.
Income of B = 60 × 450 = ₹27,000.
- **Answer: (c) ₹27,000**

**Q17.** Some students (only boys and girls) from different schools appeared for an Olympiad exam. 20% of the boys and 15% of the girls failed the exam. The number of boys who passed the exam was 70 more than that of the girls who passed the exam. A total of 90 students failed. Find the number of students that appeared for the exam.
- 1. 350 2. 420 3. 500 4. 400

**Solution:**
Let boys = b, girls = g.
Failed boys = 0.2b, Passed boys = 0.8b.
Failed girls = 0.15g, Passed girls = 0.85g.
0.8b - 0.85g = 70 ...(1)
0.2b + 0.15g = 90 ...(2)
From (2): 0.2b = 90 - 0.15g ⇒ b = 450 - 0.75g.
Sub in (1): 0.8(450 - 0.75g) - 0.85g = 70
360 - 0.6g - 0.85g = 70 ⇒ 360 - 1.45g = 70 ⇒ 1.45g = 290 ⇒ g = 200.
b = 450 - 0.75(200) = 300.
Total = 200 + 300 = 500.
- **Answer: 3. 500**

**Q18.** Some Boys and Girls from different states appeared for a CGL Exam. 20% of the boys and 16.66% of the girls failed the exam. The number of boys who passed the exam was 75 more than that of the girls who passed the exam. A total of 75 students failed. Find the number of students that appeared for the exam.
- 1. 350 2. 420 3. 500 4. 400

**Solution:**
16.66% = 1/6.
Let boys = b, girls = g.
Failed boys = 0.2b, Passed boys = 0.8b.
Failed girls = g/6, Passed girls = 5g/6.
0.8b - 5g/6 = 75 ...(1)
0.2b + g/6 = 75 ...(2)
From (2): 0.2b = 75 - g/6 ⇒ b = 375 - 5g/6.
Sub in (1): 0.8(375 - 5g/6) - 5g/6 = 75
300 - 4g/6 - 5g/6 = 75 ⇒ 300 - 9g/6 = 75 ⇒ 9g/6 = 225 ⇒ g = 150.
b = 375 - 5(150)/6 = 375 - 125 = 250.
Total = 250 + 150 = 400.
- **Answer: 4. 400**

**Q19.** In a picnic organised by a school, 2/3rd of total number of boys and 3/5 of the total number of girls got permission to go. If the total number of boys in the school is 200 more than the number of girls and number of girls who went to picnic is 160 less than the number of boys who went to the picnic. Find the total students in the school?
- 1. 800 2. 850 3. 1000 4. 1200

**Solution:**
Let boys = b, girls = g.
b = g + 200 ...(1)
Boys went = 2b/3. Girls went = 3g/5.
3g/5 = 2b/3 - 160 ...(2)
Sub (1) in (2): 3g/5 = 2(g+200)/3 - 160
3g/5 = (2g+400)/3 - 160
Multiply by 15: 9g = 5(2g+400) - 2400
9g = 10g + 2000 - 2400
9g = 10g - 400 ⇒ g = 400.
b = 400 + 200 = 600.
Total = 400 + 600 = 1000.
- **Answer: 3. 1000**
