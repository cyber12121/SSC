Unit 6 • Tenses Basics

Past ContinuousI was doing

Learn how to talk about actions in progress at a specific time in the past, and how to combine past continuous with past simple when one action interrupts another.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Form the past continuous (was/were + -ing).
  • Understand that past continuous means in the middle of doing something at a past time.
  • Compare the past continuous (in progress) with the past simple (completed action).
  • Use past continuous and past simple together (one action interrupted another).

Part A — Theory

A. Meaning of Past Continuous

Yesterday at 4 o'clock

Yesterday Karen and Jim played tennis. They started at 3 o'clock and finished at 4.30.
So at 4 o'clock, they were playing tennis.
They were playing = they were in the middle of playing. They had not finished.

was/were + -ing is the past continuous:

  • This time last year I was living in Hong Kong.
  • What were you doing at 10 o'clock last night?
  • I waved to Helen, but she wasn't looking.

B. Compare: Past Continuous vs Past Simple

Past Continuous (in the middle)

• I was walking home when I met Dan. (in the middle of walking)

• Kate was watching TV when we arrived.

Past Simple (complete action)

• I walked home after the party. (all the way, completed)

• Kate watched TV a lot while she was ill.

C. Past Simple and Past Continuous Together

We often use the past simple and past continuous together to say that something happened in the middle of something else:

• Matt phoned while we were having dinner.

• It was raining when I got up.

• I saw you in the park yesterday. You were sitting on the grass and reading a book.

Note: Some verbs (for example, know and want) are not normally used in continuous forms. See Unit 4.

• We were good friends. We knew each other well. (not we were knowing)

Summary & Quick Reference

Key Takeaways for Unit 6

Category Rule Correct Example
Past Continuous was/were + -ing for action in progress at a past time At 4 o'clock I was playing tennis.
Past Continuous + Simple Past simple interrupts past continuous The phone rang while I was cooking.
State verbs in past Always past simple (never continuous) We knew each other (not were knowing).
