Unit 8 • Tenses Basics

Present Perfect 2I have done

Learn how to discuss life experiences using Have you ever ...? and I've never ..., how to use the present perfect with unfinished time periods like today and this year, and how to express It's the first time something has happened.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use the present perfect to talk about experiences throughout your life up to now.
  • Ask and answer questions with Have you ever ...? and I've never ....
  • Use time expressions that connect with now (recently, so far, since).
  • Use the present perfect with unfinished periods (today, this week, this year).
  • Use It's the first time ... has happened.

Part A — Theory

A. Life Experience: Have you ever ...?

Have you been to France? • No, I've never been to France.
We use the present perfect when we talk about a period of time that continues from the past up to the present (our whole life).

• Have you ever eaten caviar?

• We've never had a car.

• Have you heard from Brian recently?

B. Unfinished Periods of Time

We use the present perfect with periods of time that are not finished at the time of speaking (e.g. today, this morning, this year):

• I haven't seen Tom this morning. (it is still morning)

• Have you had a holiday this year?

• I've drunk four cups of coffee today.

C. It's the first time ...

Don is having a driving lesson. It is his first one.
It's the first time he has driven a car. (not drives)
He has never driven a car before.

• Sarah has lost her passport again. This is the second time this has happened.
