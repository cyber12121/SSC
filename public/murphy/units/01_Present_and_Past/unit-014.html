Unit 14 • Tenses Basics

Present Perfect and Past 2I have done and I did

Learn the definitive rule: NEVER use the present perfect with finished time expressions like yesterday, last week, or in 2015.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Do NOT use the present perfect with finished time words (yesterday, last week, in 2015).
  • Use the past simple to ask When ...? or What time ...?.
  • Compare finished time periods with unfinished time periods.

Part A — Theory

A. Finished Time Words → Past Simple

Do not use the present perfect with finished time words (yesterday / last year / ten minutes ago / in 2010):

• It was cold yesterday. (not has been cold yesterday)

• I didn't eat any fruit yesterday. (not haven't eaten)

• She arrived at 3 o'clock. (not has arrived at 3 o'clock)

B. Compare: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Past Simple (Finished time)

• It was cold last week.

• Did you see Anna on Sunday?

• I lived in Glasgow for five years. (I don't live there now)

Present Perfect (Time up to now)

• It has been cold this week.

• Have you seen Anna recently?

• I've lived in Glasgow for five years. (I live there now)

Exercise 14.3 12 items • Put the verb into correct form

Put the verb into the correct form, present perfect or past simple.

  1. (I / not / be) to Canada, but I'd like to go there.
  2. Paul and Lucy (arrive) about ten minutes ago.
  3. I'm tired. (I / not / sleep) well last night.
  4. (There / be) a bus drivers' strike last week, so (there / be) no buses.
  5. Edward (work) in a bank for 15 years. Then (he / give) it up. Now he works as a gardener.
  6. Mary lives in Dublin. (She / live) there all her life.
  7. My grandfather (die) before I was born. (I / never / meet) him.
  8. I don't know Karen's husband. (I / never / meet) him.
  9. It's nearly lunchtime, and (I / not / see) Martin all morning. I wonder where he is.
  10. A: (you / go) to the cinema last night?
    B: Yes, but the movie (be) awful.
  11. A: (It / be) very warm here since we arrived.
    B: Yes, (it / be) 35 degrees yesterday.
  12. A: Where do you live? — B: In Boston.
    A: How long (you / live) there? — B: Five years.
    A: Where (you / live) before that? — B: In Chicago.
    A: And how long (you / live) in Chicago? — B: Two years.

Summary & Quick Reference

Key Takeaways for Unit 14

Category Rule Correct Example
Finished time words yesterday, ago, last year → ONLY Past Simple I arrived yesterday (never have arrived).
When / What time Always use past simple for When questions When did you arrive? (never When have you arrived)
