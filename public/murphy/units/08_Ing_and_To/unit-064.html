Unit 64 • -ing and to ...

to … , for … and so that …Expressing Purpose

Master expressing purpose: infinitives of purpose (to do), prepositions of purpose (for + noun vs for -ing), noun + infinitive patterns (something to eat), and purpose clauses with so that.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use the infinitive to … to express purpose (why somebody does something).
  • Form noun + infinitive phrases: something to eat, a place to park, money to buy food.
  • Distinguish between for + noun (stopped for petrol) and to + verb (stopped to get petrol).
  • Use for -ing to explain what an object is designed for (This brush is for washing dishes), avoiding it for individual actions.
  • Form purpose clauses with so that (+ modal auxiliary: can, could, will, would).

Part A — Theory

A. Infinitive of Purpose: to …

We use to … (infinitive) to explain the purpose of an action (why someone does something):

• I called the restaurant to reserve a table.
• What do you need to make bread?
• We shouted to warn everybody of the danger.
• The president has a team of bodyguards to protect him.

We also use noun + to … (something to eat, a place to park, work to do):

  • It’s hard to find a place to park in the city centre.
  • Would you like something to eat?
  • Is there a chair to sit on?
  • They gave us money to buy food.

B. for … vs. to …

for + noun
• We stopped for petrol.
• I had to run for the bus.
• Can you lend me money for a taxi?
to + verb
• We stopped to get petrol.
• I had to run to catch the bus.
• Can you lend me money to pay for a taxi?
What something is used for: for -ing • This brush is for washing the dishes. (function of an object)
Compare: I went into the kitchen to wash the dishes. (not for washing)

C. Purpose Clauses: so that

We use so that especially when the sentence involves modals (can/could, will/would):

  • She’s learning English so that she can study in Canada.
  • We moved to London so that we could see our friends more often.
  • I hurried so that I wouldn’t be late. (or … so I wouldn't be late)
