Unit 54 • -ing and to ...

Verb + to …decide to … forget to … etc.

Learn the essential verbs followed by the infinitive with to, continuous and perfect infinitives (to be doing, to have done), and question words + to ….

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Recognize verbs followed by to …: offer, agree, refuse, decide, plan, arrange, hope, forget, manage, fail, promise, threaten, deserve, afford, learn, tend.
  • Form negatives correctly with not to … (e.g. decided not to go).
  • Use continuous (to be doing) and perfect infinitives (to have done) with seem, appear, claim, pretend.
  • Master question words + to … (how to get, what to do, whether to go).

Part A — Theory

A. Verbs Followed by to … (Infinitive)

After these verbs you can use to …:

offer
agree
refuse
decide
plan
arrange
hope
forget
manage
fail
promise
threaten
deserve
afford
learn
tend
  • It was a long way to walk, so we decided to take a taxi home.
  • Simon was in a difficult situation, so I agreed to help him.
  • I waved to Karen, but failed to attract her attention.
  • I like Dan, but I think he tends to talk too much.
  • How old were you when you learnt to drive? (or … learnt how to drive?)
Negative Form: not to … • We decided not to go out because of the weather.
• I promised not to be late.

B. Continuous & Perfect Infinitives with Seem, Appear, Pretend, Claim

After verbs like seem, appear, pretend and claim, you can use:

  • Simple infinitive: They seem to have plenty of money.
  • Continuous infinitive (to be -ing): I pretended to be reading the newspaper. (= I pretended that I was reading)
  • Perfect infinitive (to have done): Have you seen my keys? I seem to have lost them. (= it seems that I have lost them)
  • She claimed not to have seen me. (= she claimed that she hadn't seen me)

C. Question Words + to … (what/how/where to …)

After verbs like ask, know, decide, remember, forget, learn, explain, understand, wonder, we can use what/how/where/whether + to …:

• We asked how to get to the station.
• Have you decided where to go on holiday?
• I don’t know whether to apply for the job or not.
• Do you understand what to do?
• Can somebody show me how to use this camera?
