Unit 62 • -ing and to ...

Verb + preposition + -ingsucceed in -ing / insist on -ing etc.

Master verbs requiring specific prepositions before -ing (approve of, decide against, dream of, feel like, insist on, look forward to, succeed in, think of), and structures with verb + object + preposition + -ing.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Pair common verbs with their obligatory prepositions followed by -ing: succeed in, insist on, think of/about, dream of, approve of, decide against, feel like, look forward to.
  • Master the structure verb + object + preposition + -ing: accuse of, congratulate on, prevent from, stop from, suspect of, thank for.
  • Form negatives correctly with preposition + not -ing (e.g. accused me of not telling the truth).
  • Use passive constructions: was accused of lying, was suspected of being a spy.

Part A — Theory

A. Verb + Preposition + -ing

If a verb is followed by a preposition and another verb, the second verb must end in -ing:

approve of: He doesn't approve of swearing.
decide against: We decided against moving.
dream of: I wouldn't dream of asking for money.
feel like: I don't feel like going out tonight.
insist on: They insisted on paying for the meal.
look forward to: Are you looking forward to going away?
succeed in: Has Paul succeeded in finding a job?
think of/about: I’m thinking of buying a house.

B. Verb + Object + Preposition + -ing

These verbs take an object before the preposition and -ing:

  • accuse somebody of: He accused me of telling lies. (or … of not telling the truth)
  • congratulate somebody on: We congratulated Lisa on winning the first prize.
  • prevent / stop somebody from: What prevented you from coming to see us? / The rain didn't stop us (from) enjoying our holiday.
  • suspect somebody of: Nobody suspected the general of being a spy.
  • thank somebody for: I thanked everyone for helping me.
  • apologise to somebody for: I apologised to them for keeping them waiting.
