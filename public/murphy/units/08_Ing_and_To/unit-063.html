Unit 63 • -ing and to ...

there's no point in -ing, it's worth -ing etc.Expressions followed by -ing

Master popular colloquial structures followed by -ing: there's no point in, it's no use, it's worth, have trouble/difficulty, spend/waste time, and outdoor activities with go -ing.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use there's no point in -ing, it's no use -ing, and it's no good -ing.
  • Form sentences with it's worth -ing (e.g. It's worth seeing) and it's worth it.
  • Use have trouble / difficulty / a problem + -ing.
  • Use spend / waste time doing something and be busy doing.
  • Apply go -ing for sports and recreational pursuits (go skiing, go shopping).

Part A — Theory

A. there’s no point in / it’s no use / it’s worth -ing

These common expressions are followed by -ing:

• there’s no point in: There’s no point in having a car if you never use it.
• it’s no use: It’s no use worrying about what happened.
• it’s no good: It’s no good trying to persuade me. You won’t succeed.
• it’s worth: It’s a great movie. It’s worth seeing. / It was early, so it wasn't worth going to bed.

Note: We say there's no point in having, but what's the point of having.

B. have trouble / difficulty / problems doing something

We say have trouble / difficulty / a problem + -ing (not to do):

  • I had no trouble finding a place to stay. (not trouble to find)
  • Did you have a problem getting a visa?
  • People sometimes have difficulty reading my handwriting.

C. spend / waste time & go -ing

spend / waste (time) doing:

  • He spent hours trying to repair the clock.
  • I waste a lot of time doing nothing.
  • She was too busy doing other things.
go -ing (Sports & Recreation) We use go -ing for sports and activities:
go swimming, go sailing, go fishing, go skiing, go riding, go hiking, go surfing, go scuba diving, go jogging, go camping, go shopping.
