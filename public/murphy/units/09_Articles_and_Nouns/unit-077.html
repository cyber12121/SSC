Unit 77 • Articles and nouns

Names with and without the 1Continents, countries, states, islands, cities, mountains, regions

We do not use 'the' with names of people or most names of places (continents, countries, cities, mountains). But we use 'the' with Republic, Kingdom, States, island groups, mountain ranges, and rivers/seas/oceans.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Learn when NOT to use the: names of people, continents, most countries/states, islands, cities, and single mountains.
  • Learn when to use the: countries with Republic, Kingdom, States (the United States, the UK).
  • Identify plural place names with the: island groups (the Seychelles, the Bahamas) and mountain ranges (the Alps, the Rockies).
  • Master geographical bodies of water: oceans, seas, rivers, and canals take the (the Atlantic, the Nile, the Mediterranean).

Part A — Theory

Part A: Continents, Countries, Cities, Mountains (No 'the')

We do not use the with names of people (Helen, Helen Taylor etc.). In the same way, we do not use the with most names of places:

Continents: Africa, Asia, Europe, South America
Countries, states: France, Japan, Germany, Texas, Brazil
Islands: Sicily, Bermuda, Tasmania
Cities, towns: Cairo, Bangkok, Paris, New York
Mountains (single): Everest, Kilimanjaro, Ben Nevis
Important Exceptions We normally use the in names with Republic, Kingdom, States:
the Czech Republic • the United Kingdom (the UK) • the United States of America (the USA) • the Dominican Republic

Part B: Plural Names (the Netherlands, the Alps)

We use the + plural names of countries, island groups, and mountain ranges:

Countries (plural): the Netherlands, the Philippines
Island groups: the Canaries (the Canary Islands), the Bahamas, the Seychelles
Mountain ranges: the Rocky Mountains (the Rockies), the Andes, the Alps, the Himalayas

Compare: Everest (one mountain, no the) vs the Himalayas (mountain range, with the).

Part C: Oceans, Seas, Rivers, Canals (With 'the')

We use the with names of oceans, seas, rivers, and canals:

Oceans: the Atlantic, the Pacific, the Indian Ocean
Seas: the Mediterranean, the Red Sea, the Black Sea
Rivers: the Nile, the Amazon, the Thames, the Danube, the Rhine
Canals: the Suez Canal, the Panama Canal

Lakes: We do not normally use the with names of lakes: Lake Superior, Lake Michigan, Lake Victoria.

Part D: Compass Directions & Titles

We say the north / the south / the east / the west / the middle (of ...):

Milan is in the north of Italy.
Compare: northern Italy (adjective without the), South Africa, southeast Asia.

Titles with names: We do not use the when a title is followed by a person's name:

Doctor Thomas • Professor Brown • President Kennedy • Inspector Roberts • Queen Elizabeth
Compare: the Doctor or the President (without the person's name).
