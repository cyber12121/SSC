Unit 73 • Articles and nouns

the 1Unique things, superlatives, the sky/the sea, media and meals

We use 'the' when there is only one of something, with superlatives, and with unique features of our world. But notice exceptions like 'space' and meals.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Learn when to use the because there is only one of something (the equator, the tenth floor, the capital).
  • Master the + superlative (the longest river, the best way).
  • Identify unique nouns: the sun, the moon, the world, the sky, the sea, the ground, the internet, and contrast with space (without the).
  • Learn rules for media (the radio vs television/TV), meals (breakfast/lunch/dinner without the), and numbered places (Gate 24, page 15, platform 3).

Part A — Theory

Part A: Only One of Something; Superlatives

We use the when there is only one of something:

Have you ever crossed the equator? (there is only one equator)
Our apartment is on the tenth floor.
Buenos Aires is the capital of Argentina.
I’m going away at the end of this month.

We use the + superlative (best, oldest, longest etc.):

What’s the longest river in Europe?
What’s the best way to learn a language?

the (unique / specific):
The sun is a star.
The hotel where we stayed was very old.
We live in an apartment on the tenth floor.

a / an (one of many):
The sun is a star. (= one of many stars)
We stayed at a very old hotel.
We live in an apartment on the tenth floor.

Part B: The Sun / The Moon / The Sky vs Space

We say the sun / the moon / the world / the sky / the sea / the ground / the environment / the internet:

I’d like to travel around the world.
Look at the sky. It’s going to rain.
The internet is a great source of information.
Do you live near the sea?

Warning: We do not use the with space (= the universe outside Earth):

There are millions of stars in space. (not in the space)
I’d love to travel in space.

Part C: The Same; The Radio vs Television

We say the same:

These two photos are the same.
We arrived at the same time.

We say the radio, but usually television / TV (without the):

I listen to the radio a lot.
We watch television / TV a lot.
Note: Can you turn off the television? (= the television set / physical appliance)

Part D: Meals & Noun + Number

We do not usually use the with the names of meals (breakfast / lunch / dinner):

What did you have for breakfast? (not for the breakfast)
We had lunch in a restaurant.
Compare: We had a very nice lunch. (with an adjective, we use a/an)

We do not use the before noun + number:

Our flight leaves from Gate 24. (not the Gate 24)
You’ll find the answer on page 15.
The train leaves from platform 3.
Compare: the 24th gate, the third platform.
