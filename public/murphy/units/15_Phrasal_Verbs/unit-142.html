Unit 142 • Phrasal verbs

Phrasal verbs 6: up/downComparing up and down, down = getting less, and other verbs with down

Contrast directional up and down, and explore verbs like knock down, burn down, calm down, cut down on, let down, and turn down.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Compare physical directional pairs: put up / take down, pick up / put down, stand up / sit down, turn up / turn down.
  • Use down for destruction and felling: knock down, cut down, blow down, burn down.
  • Use down for slowing, reducing, calming, disappointing, and recording: slow down, calm down, cut down on, close down, let down, write down.
  • Master break down (stop working) and turn down (refuse an offer/application).

Part A — Theory

Part A: Comparing Up and Down

up

put something up (on a wall etc.):
I put a picture up on the wall.

pick something up (from floor/table):
There was a letter on the floor. I picked it up.

stand up:
Alan stood up and walked out.

turn something up:
I can’t hear the TV. Can you turn it up a bit?

down

take something down (from a wall etc.):
I didn’t like the picture, so I took it down.

put something down:
I stopped writing and put down my pen.

sit down / bend down / lie down:
I bent down to tie my shoelace.

turn something down:
The oven is too hot. Turn it down to 150 degrees.

Part B: Knock Down, Cut Down, Burn Down

knock down a building / blow down / cut down:
Some old houses were knocked down to make way for the new shopping centre.
Why did you cut down the tree in your garden?
A few trees were blown down in the storm.
be knocked down (by a vehicle):
A man was knocked down by a car and taken to hospital.
burn down = be destroyed by fire:
They put out the fire before the house burnt down.

Part C: Down = Getting Less, Slower, Calmer

slow down = go more slowly:
You’re driving too fast. Slow down.
calm down = become calmer, make someone calmer:
Calm down. There’s no point in getting angry.
cut down (on something) = consume or do less often:
I’m trying to cut down on coffee. I drink too much of it.
close down / shut down = stop operating permanently:
There used to be a shop here. It closed down a few years ago.

Part D: Other Verbs with Down

let somebody down = disappoint someone by failing to keep a promise/expectation:
You can always rely on Paul. He’ll never let you down.
write something down = record information on paper:
I can’t remember Ben’s address. I wrote it down, but I can’t find it.
break down = stop working (machines, cars, relationships):
The car broke down and I had to phone for help.
Their marriage broke down after only a few months.
turn somebody/something down = refuse/reject an offer, job, or application:
I applied for several jobs, but I was turned down for all of them.
Rachel was offered the job, but she decided to turn it down.
