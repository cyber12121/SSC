Unit 36 • Tenses Basics

wouldHypothetical situations, past refusal, past habits

Learn how to use would for imagined situations, unfulfilled past events with would have, and past habits.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use would ('d) to imagine a situation or action in hypothetical situations.
  • Use would have (done) to talk about imagined past situations that did not occur.
  • Use would / wouldn't for past refusal and past habitual actions.

Part A — Theory

A. would in hypothetical situations

We use would ('d) when we imagine a situation or action (we think of something that is not real):

  • It would be nice to buy a new car, but we can't afford it.
  • I'd love to live by the sea.
  • What would you do if you won a lot of money?

B. would have (done) (Past)

We use would have (done) when we imagine situations or actions in the past (things that did not happen):

• They helped us a lot. I don't know what we would have done without their help.

• It's a shame you couldn't come to the party. You would have enjoyed it.

C. would for past refusal and past habits

wouldn't (past refusal)

• I asked him what happened, but he wouldn't tell me. (= refused to tell)

• The car wouldn't start this morning.

would (past habits = used to)

• Whenever we visited our grandparents, they would give us sweets.

• He was a friendly man. He would always smile when he saw you.

Key Takeaways for Unit 36

Category Rule Correct Example

would have (past) Imagining past events that did not occur You would have enjoyed the party (if you had come).
