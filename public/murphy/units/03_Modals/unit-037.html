Unit 37 • Tenses Basics

can/could/would you …? etc.Requests, offers, permission, and invitations

Learn how to make polite requests, ask for permission, and make gracious offers and invitations.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Make polite requests with Can you ... ? / Could you ... ? / Would you ... ?
  • Ask for things and permission with Can I have ... ? / Could I ... ? / May I ... ?
  • Make offers and invitations with Would you like ... ? / Can I ... ?

Part A — Theory

A. Asking people to do things (Requests)

We use can or could to ask people to do things:

  • Can you wait a moment, please?
  • Could you pass the salt, please? (more polite than can)

You can also use Would you ... ? or Do you think you could ... ?:

  • Do you think you could lend me some money?
  • Do you mind turning the music down?

B. Asking for things and permission

  • Asking for things: Can I have the bill, please? / Could we have some water?
  • Asking permission: Can I use your phone? / Could I leave early? / May I come in?
  • Is it OK if ... ?: Is it OK if I close the window? / Do you mind if I sit here?

C. Offering and inviting

  • Offering: Can I help you? / Can I give you a hand?
  • Inviting: Would you like a cup of coffee? / Would you like to come to the cinema?
