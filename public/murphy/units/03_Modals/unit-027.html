Unit 27 • Tenses Basics

could (do) and could have (done)Suggestions, past opportunities, hypothetical ability

Learn how to use could for present possibilities and could have done for unfulfilled opportunities in the past.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use could as a less definite alternative to can for present suggestions and possibilities.
  • Use could have (done) for past opportunities that were possible but didn't happen.
  • Differentiate between couldn't (do) and couldn't have (done).

Part A — Theory

A. could for present suggestions and possibilities

We use could in a number of ways. Sometimes could is the past of can (Unit 26). But could is also used for present or future suggestions and possibilities:

  • 'What shall we do tonight?' 'We could go to the cinema.'
  • When you go to Paris next month, you could stay with Sarah.

can is also possible in these sentences (We can go to the cinema). Could is less sure than can.

B. could have (done)

We use could have (done) to talk about the past — to say that something was possible, but did not happen:

• Why did you stay at a hotel? You could have stayed with Sarah. (you didn't stay with her)

• David was lucky. He could have hurt himself when he fell, but he's OK.

C. couldn't and couldn't have

couldn't (present)

• I couldn't live in a big city. I'd hate it. (= it wouldn't be possible for me)

• Everything is fine. Things couldn't be better.

couldn't have (past)

• We had a really good holiday. It couldn't have been better.

• You helped me a lot. I couldn't have managed without you.
