Unit 123 • Prepositions

in/at/on (position) 1in a room/country; at a point/bus stop; on a surface/island

Master the essential principles of position prepositions: in for enclosed spaces, containers, and geographic areas; at for specific points, events, and stops; and on for surfaces, lines, and islands.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use in for 3D enclosed spaces, containers, rooms, buildings, towns, and countries.
  • Use at for points along a journey, bus stops, doors, desks, and specific meeting points.
  • Use on for surfaces (floor, wall, ceiling, page), islands, and attached items.
  • Compare nuanced differences: in the shop (inside building) vs at the shop (as a landmark / point on a route); in the bottle vs on the bottle (label).

Part A — Theory

Part A: in • Enclosed Space / Area

in is used when something is inside a 3D space, container, building, or geographical area:

• in a room / in a building / in a box / in a garden
• in a town / in a country / in the city centre
• in a pool / in the sea / in a river
• There's nobody in the room. • What have you got in your bag?
• Milan is in the north of Italy.

Part B: at • A Point, Stop, or Landmark

at is used for a point in space, a bus stop, an event, or a stop along a journey:

• at the bus stop / at the door / at the roundabout / at her desk
• at the traffic lights / at the reception desk
• There's somebody at the door. Shall I go and see who it is?
• Turn left at the traffic lights.
in vs at a building

• There were a lot of people in the shop. (inside the building)
• Go along this road, then turn left at the shop. (a point/landmark)
• I'll meet you in the lobby vs at the entrance.

at vs on the door

• There is somebody at the door. (standing by it)
• There is a notice on the door. (attached to the surface)
• The key is in the door. (inside the lock)

Part C: on • Surfaces, Pages, and Islands

on is used for contact with a horizontal or vertical surface, or an island:

• on the floor / on the ground / on the grass / on the beach
• on the ceiling / on your nose / on your shirt / on the wall
• on page seven of the newspaper • on an island
• There is some water in the bottle, but a label on the bottle.
