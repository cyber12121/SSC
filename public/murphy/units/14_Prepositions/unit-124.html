Unit 124 • Prepositions

in/at/on (position) 2in a row/sky; at the top/back; on the left/second floor

Explore further positional patterns: in lines, printed media, the sky/world; at top/bottom, front/back of buildings and groups; and on sides (left/right), building floors, maps, menus, and waterways.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use in with lines/rows/queues, pictures, newspapers/books, departments/offices, the sky, the world, the country.
  • Use at with the top / the bottom / the end of ... and at the front / back of a building/cinema/group.
  • Compare: in the front / back of a car vs at the front / back of a building vs on the front / back of a letter/card.
  • Use in the corner (of a room) vs at / on the corner (of a street).
  • Use on for sides (on the left/right), floors (on the second floor), maps, menus, lists, websites, and bodies of water (on the coast / on a river).

Part A — Theory

Part A: Fixed Phrases with in

• in a line / in a row / in a queue: They're standing in a row. When I go to the cinema, I like to sit in the front row.
• in a picture / in a photo(graph): Who is the woman in that picture?
• in a newspaper / in a magazine / in a book: I read about it in the newspaper.
• in an office / in a department: Amy works in the sales department.
• in the sky / in the world: There isn't a cloud in the sky. What is the tallest building in the world?
• in the country (= not in a town): Do you live in a city or in the country?

Part B: Fixed Phrases with on

• on the left / on the right (or on the left-hand / right-hand side): In Britain people drive on the left.
• on the ground / first / second floor: Our flat is on the second floor of the building.
• on a map / on a menu / on a list / on a website: Don't buy anything that's not on the shopping list.
• on a river / on the coast / on a road: Vienna is on the Danube. The town is on the coast.
• on the way (from one place to another): We stopped at a shop on the way home.

Part C: at the top / bottom / end of ...

• at the top (of ...) / at the bottom (of ...): Write your name at the top of the page.
• at the end (of ...): Jane lives at the other end of the street.

Part D: front and back • in, at, or on?

in the front / back (of a car)

Used specifically for vehicles:
I was in the back of the car when we had the accident.

at the front / back (building/room)

For buildings, theatres, cinemas, classes:
The garden is at the back of the house. Let's sit at the front of the cinema.

• on the front / back (of an envelope, photo, card): I wrote the date on the back of the photo.

Part E: corner • in, at, or on?

• in the corner (of a room): The TV is in the corner of the room.
• at the corner OR on the corner (of a street): There is a small shop at/on the corner of the street.
