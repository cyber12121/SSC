Unit 125 • Prepositions

in/at/on (position) 3at hospital/work; in bed/hospital; on a bus/train vs in a car/taxi

Master position prepositions for institutions, public venues, and transport: at vs in for hospital, school, university, events, and stations; and on vs in for public transit vs private cars and taxis.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use at with buildings/institutions when thinking of their activity: at home, at work, at school, at university, at the station, at the airport.
  • Use at an event: at a party, at a concert, at a conference, at a meeting, at a football match.
  • Compare in hospital / prison / bed (as a patient/prisoner/sleeper) vs at the hospital (as a visitor).
  • Use on for public transport and two-wheelers (on a bus, on a train, on a plane, on a bike) vs in for cars and taxis (in a car, in a taxi).

Part A — Theory

Part A: at home, at work, at school etc.

• at home / at work / at school / at university / at college:
I'll be at work until 5.30. • My sister is at university in London.
• at the station / at the airport:
I'll meet you at the airport. • Does this train stop at Oxford?

Part B: at an event

We use at for events and social gatherings:
• at a party / at a concert / at a conference / at a meeting / at a sports match
Were there many people at the concert? • I saw Karen at a conference last week.

Part C: in bed / in hospital / in prison

• in bed: Mark was feeling ill, so he stayed in bed.
• in hospital / in prison (as a patient or prisoner):
Two people were injured in the accident and are still in hospital.
Some people are in prison for crimes they did not commit.
(Note: We say 'at the hospital' when visiting: 'I went to visit Martin at the hospital.')

Part D: Transport • on vs in

on • Public transport & two-wheelers

on a bus • on a train
on a plane • on a ship
on a bike • on a motorbike • on a horse
The bus was full. There were too many people on it.
Jane passed me on her bike.

in • Cars and taxis

in a car • in a taxi
Laura arrived in a taxi.
Shall we travel in your car or mine?
