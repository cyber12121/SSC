Unit 132 • Prepositions

Verb + preposition 1to and at: talk to, explain to, laugh at, shout at/to, throw at/to

Master verbs taking to and at: talk/listen/apologise/explain to somebody vs verbs that take no preposition (phone/call/ask/answer somebody); look/laugh/aim/point at; and verbs whose meaning changes with at vs to (shout at [angry] vs shout to [to be heard]; throw at [to hit] vs throw to [to catch]).

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use verb + to: talk to, speak to, listen to, apologise to somebody (for something), explain something to somebody.
  • Avoid to with verbs that take a direct object: phone somebody, call somebody, text somebody, email somebody, ask somebody, answer somebody/something.
  • Use verb + at: look at, stare at, glance at, laugh at, aim at, point something at.
  • Contrast shout at (angry) with shout to (so they can hear you).
  • Contrast throw something at (to hit) with throw something to (for somebody to catch).

Part A — Theory

Part A: verb + TO

• talk / speak to somebody (talk with is also possible): Who were you talking to?
• listen to ...: When I'm driving, I like to listen to the radio. (not listen the radio)
• apologise to somebody (for something): They apologised to me for their mistake. (not apologised me)
• explain something to somebody: Can you explain this word to me? (not explain me this word)
• explain / describe to somebody what/why: I explained to them why I was worried.

Part B: Verbs WITHOUT to (Direct Objects)

No preposition

• phone / call / text / email somebody:
I called the airline. (not called to the airline)
• ask somebody:
You can ask me. (not ask to me)
• answer somebody / something:
You didn't answer my email. (not answer to)

With preposition

• write (a letter) to somebody
• reply to an email / letter

Part C: verb + AT

• look / stare / glance at ... • have / take a look at ...: Why are you looking at me like that?
• laugh at ...: I look stupid. Everybody will laugh at me.
• aim / point (something) at ...: Don't point that knife at me. It's dangerous.
• shoot / fire at ...: We saw someone shooting at birds.

Part D: at vs to • shout and throw

shout at vs shout to

• shout at (angry/aggressive):
He got very angry and started shouting at me.
• shout to (so they can hear you):
He shouted to me from the other side of the street.

throw at vs throw to

• throw something at (to hit them):
Somebody threw an egg at the politician.
• throw something to (for them to catch):
Lisa shouted 'Catch!' and threw the keys to me.
