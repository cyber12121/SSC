Unit 127 • Prepositions

in/on/at (other uses)in love/cold weather; on holiday/TV/strike; at 100 degrees/21

Master high-frequency fixed idioms and state expressions: in conditions, mood, and languages; on media, operations, and excursions; and at ages, temperatures, speeds, and rates.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use in for weather/conditions (in the rain, in cold weather, in the shade, in the dark), languages (in French), feelings/states (in love, in a good mood), and opinion (in my opinion).
  • Use on for trips/plans (on holiday, on business, on a cruise, on a trip/tour), media (on TV, on the radio, on the phone), states (on strike, on fire, on a diet), and intention (on purpose, on the whole).
  • Use at for measurements and points on a scale: at 100 degrees, at 50 miles an hour, at the age of 21, at speed.

Part A — Theory

Part A: Common Expressions with in

• in the rain / in the sun / in the shade / in the dark / in cold weather:
Don't go out in the rain. • It's too hot here. I'm going to sit in the shade.
• in (a language): The book is written in French. • What's that in English?
• in ink / in pencil / in capital letters: Please write in ink.
• in love (with somebody): They fell in love almost immediately.
• in (a good/bad) mood: I don't feel like going out. I'm not in the mood.
• in my opinion: In my opinion, it wasn't a good film.

Part B: Common Expressions with on

• on holiday / on business / on a trip / on a tour / on a cruise:
Jane is away on business. • We went on a tour of the city.
• on television (TV) / on the radio / on the phone:
I heard it on the radio. • Is there anything worth watching on TV?
• on strike / on fire / on a diet:
Workers have gone on strike. • The building was on fire.
• on purpose (= intentionally) • on the whole (= in general):
I didn't do it on purpose. • On the whole, the examination was OK.

Part C: Common Expressions with at

We use at for speeds, temperatures, ages, and points on a numerical scale:
• at 100 degrees Celsius / at 120 km/h: Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
• at the age of 21 / at 21: David got married at 19.
• at (great) speed: The car was travelling at high speed.
