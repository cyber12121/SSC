Unit 136 • Prepositions

Verb + preposition 5 in/into/with/to/onBelieve in vs believe; into divisions and crashes; collide/fill/provide with; prefer to; insist/concentrate on

Discover verbs that link with 'in' (specialise in), 'into' (divide into), 'with' (provide with), 'to' (prefer A to B), and 'on' (concentrate on).

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Master verbs taking in: believe in (existence / principle), specialise in, succeed in.
  • Distinguish between believe in (faith / principle) and believe (accept as true).
  • Master verbs taking into: break into, divide/split into, crash/drive/bump into, translate from... into.
  • Master verbs taking with: collide with, fill with, provide/supply somebody with.
  • Master verbs taking to: happen to, invite to, prefer A to B.
  • Master verbs taking on: concentrate on, spend money on, insist on.

Part A — Theory

Part A: Verb + In and Believe Distinction

verb + in:

specialise IN... • Helen specialises in company law.
succeed IN... • I hope you succeed in finding the job you want.
believe IN... = believe that something exists, or that it is right/good to do:
Do you believe in God? • I believe in telling the truth.
Believe IN (Existence / Principle)

Do you believe in ghosts?

I don’t believe in wasting money.

Believe (Direct Object: Truth / Person)

The story can’t be true. I don’t believe it. (not believe in it)

I don’t believe him. (= I think he is lying)

Part B: Verb + Into and Verb + With

verb + into (entry, division, collision, translation):

break INTO... • Our house was broken into.
divide / cut / split something INTO parts • The book is divided into three parts.
crash / drive / bump / run INTO... • He crashed into a wall.
translate FROM one language INTO another • translated into many languages

verb + with:

collide WITH... • A bus collided with a car.
fill something WITH... • Fill the saucepan with water.
provide / supply somebody WITH... • The school provides students with books.

Part C: Verb + To and Verb + On

Verb + To

happen TO...
What happened to that watch you had?

invite somebody TO a party / wedding
invited to their wedding

prefer one thing TO another
I prefer tea to coffee.

Verb + On

concentrate ON...
I tried to concentrate on my work.

spend (money / time) ON...
How much do you spend on food?

insist ON...
They insisted on coming with me.
