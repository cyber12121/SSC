Unit 128 • Prepositions

byby mistake/car; passive agent; beside/next to; margin of difference

Explore the versatile preposition by: for method and transport (by car, by email), agency in passives (by Shakespeare), proximity (by the door = beside), and margins of difference (increased by 10%).

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use by + noun without articles for methods: by mistake, by accident, by chance, by post, by email, by hand vs on purpose.
  • Use by for transport modes: by car, by train, by bus, by plane, by bike vs in my car, on the train, on foot.
  • Distinguish the passive agent (by + person/force) from instruments (with + tool): opened by someone with a key.
  • Use by to mean 'beside / next to': sit by me, the table by the bed.
  • Use by to express margins of change or difference: won by five metres, increased by 6 million.

Part A — Theory

Part A: by for Methods & Fixed Expressions

• by mistake / by accident / by chance (no article):
We hadn't arranged to meet. We met by chance.
• Contrast with on purpose (= intentionally): I didn't do it on purpose. It was an accident.
• by post / by email / by hand / by credit card / by cheque:
Can I pay by credit card? (but note: pay cash or pay in cash).

Part B: Transport • by vs in / on

by + noun (general means)

by car / by train / by bus
by plane / by boat / by bike
Jess usually goes to work by bus / by bike / by car.

in / on + determiner

in my car / in a taxi (not by my car)
on the train / on the 6.45 bus
on foot (walk, not by foot)

Part C: Agency • by vs with

In passive sentences, by indicates who or what performed the action; with indicates the instrument used:

• Have you ever been bitten by a dog?
• The door must have been opened by somebody with a key. (not by a key)
• We put the fire out with a fire extinguisher.
• a play by Shakespeare • a painting by Rembrandt • music by Beethoven

Part D: Proximity & Margins of Difference

by = next to / beside

• The light switch is by the door.
• Come and sit by me.
• They live in a house by the sea.

by = margin of difference

• Clare's salary has increased by ten per cent.
• Carl won the race by five metres.
• Helen won by two votes.
