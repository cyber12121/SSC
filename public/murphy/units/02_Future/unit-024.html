Unit 24 • Tenses Basics

will be doing and will have doneFuture continuous and future perfect

Learn how to talk about actions in progress in the future (will be doing) and actions completed before a future time (will have done).

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Form and use the future continuous: will be doing (in progress at a future moment).
  • Form and use the future perfect: will have done (completed before a future moment).
  • Use will be doing for routine or already planned future events.

Part A — Theory

A. will be doing (future continuous)

Study this example situation:

Andy goes to work every day. He leaves home at 8 o'clock and arrives at work at about 8.45. He starts work immediately and continues until 12.30 when he has lunch. He starts work again at 1.15 and goes home at exactly 4.30.
• At 10 o'clock tomorrow, he will be working.
• At 12.45 tomorrow, he will be having lunch.

will be doing something = in the middle of doing something in the future:

  • Don't phone between 7 and 8. We'll be eating then.
  • Let's wait for Liz to arrive and then we'll have dinner. (vs At 8 o'clock we'll be having dinner.)

We also use will be -ing for complete actions in the future (events that are part of a routine or schedule):

  • I'll be going to the supermarket later. Can I get you anything?

B. will have done (future perfect)

Andy leaves work at 4.30. So at 5 o'clock tomorrow, he will have finished work.

will have done something = it will already be complete before a specific time in the future:

  • Sally always leaves for work at 8.30 in the morning. She won't be at home at 9 o'clock – she will have gone to work.
  • We're late. The film will already have started by the time we get to the cinema.

Summary & Quick Reference

Key Takeaways for Unit 24

Category Rule Correct Example
Future Continuous will be + -ing (in the middle of doing at future time) Don't phone at 7.30. We'll be having dinner.
Future Perfect will have + past participle (completed before future time) By 11 o'clock, the meeting will have finished.
Routine Events will be doing for planned routine actions I'll be going shopping later. Can I get you anything?
