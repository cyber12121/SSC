Unit 43 • Passive

Passive 2 (be done / been done / being done)Continuous passives, perfect passives, and modal infinitives

Explore continuous passive structures, perfect tenses in the passive, and modal auxiliary passive infinitives.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Master the continuous passive forms (is being done / was being done).
  • Form perfect passive sentences with have/has been done and had been done.
  • Use modal auxiliaries with passive infinitives (can be done, must be done).
  • Form perfect modal passives for past deductions and regrets (should have been done).

Part A — Theory

A. Present Perfect & Past Perfect Passive

Present Perfect Passive

have/has been + past participle

  • The room looks nice. It has been cleaned.
  • Have you heard? The trip has been cancelled.
  • Have you ever been bitten by a dog?
  • ‘Are you going to the party?’ ‘No, I haven't been invited.’
Past Perfect Passive

had been + past participle

  • The room looked nice. It had been cleaned.
  • The vegetables didn't taste good. They had been cooked too long.
  • The car was three years old, but hadn't been used very much.

B. Present Continuous & Past Continuous Passive

Present Continuous Passive

am/is/are + being + past participle

  • Somebody is cleaning the room → The room is being cleaned at the moment.
  • There's somebody walking behind us. I think we are being followed.
  • A new bridge is being built across the river.
Past Continuous Passive

was/were + being + past participle

  • Somebody was cleaning the room → The room was being cleaned when I arrived.
  • There was somebody walking behind us. I think we were being followed.

C. Passive Infinitives (be done / have been done)

Infinitive: (to) be + done
  • The room will be cleaned later.
  • The situation is serious. Something must be done.
  • A mystery is something that can't be explained.
  • A new supermarket is going to be built next year.
  • Please go away. I want to be left alone.
Perfect Infinitive: (to) have been + done
  • The room should have been cleaned.
  • The letter might have been sent to the wrong address.
  • If you had locked the car, it wouldn't have been stolen.
  • There were problems at first, but they seem to have been solved.

Answers with Full Explanations

Exercise 43.1 — Key & Explanations
  1. must be done (example)
  2. might have been sent (example)
  3. be made — future passive infinitive after will not.
  4. be kept — modal passive after should always.
  5. have been repaired — past modal passive after should.
  6. be carried — passive infinitive after had to.
  7. have been arrested — third conditional passive after wouldn't.
  8. be delayed — future possibility passive after may.
  9. have been caused — past possibility passive after might.
  10. be knocked — passive infinitive after going to.
  11. be known — future passive after will.
  12. have been forgotten — perfect passive infinitive after seems to.
Exercise 43.2 — Key & Explanations
  1. We're being followed. (example)
  2. Have you painted it? (example)
  3. It's been stolen! / It has been stolen! — present perfect passive.
  4. Somebody has taken it (or my umbrella). — active.
  5. He hasn't been seen since then. — present perfect passive.
  6. I haven't seen her for ages. — active.
  7. Have you ever been stung by a bee? — present perfect passive question.
  8. It's / It is being repaired at the moment. — present continuous passive.
  9. It hasn't / has not been found yet. — present perfect passive negative.
  10. The furniture had been moved. — past perfect passive.
Exercise 43.3 — Key & Explanations
  1. The room has been cleaned. (example)
  2. A new road is being built around the city. — present continuous passive.
  3. Two new hotels have been built near the airport. — present perfect passive.
  4. some new houses were being built. — past continuous passive.
  5. The date of the meeting has been changed. — present perfect passive.
  6. our conversation was being recorded. — past continuous passive.
  7. Is anything being done about the problem? — present continuous passive question.
  8. They hadn't / had not been cleaned for ages. — past perfect passive negative.
