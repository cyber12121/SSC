Unit 98 • Adjectives and adverbs

Adjectives ending in -ing and -ed (boring/bored etc.)-ing (describing a thing/person causing the feeling) vs -ed (describing how someone feels)

Many adjectives have both -ing and -ed forms. An -ing adjective describes the person, thing, or situation that causes a feeling, while an -ed adjective expresses how someone feels.

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Distinguish between participial adjectives ending in -ing and -ed.
  • Use -ing adjectives (boring, interesting, tiring) to describe what something is like or the effect it produces.
  • Use -ed adjectives (bored, interested, tired) to express personal feelings and emotional reactions.
  • Avoid the common error of saying I am boring when you mean I am bored.

Part A — Theory

Part A: The Core Distinction: -ing vs -ed

Study this example situation:

Jane has been doing the same job for a very long time. Every day she does exactly the same thing again and again. She doesn't enjoy her job any more and would like to do something different.

• Jane's job is boring.
• Jane is bored with her job.

Somebody is bored or gets bored if something (or somebody else) is boring. If something is boring, you get bored with it.

Jane is bored because her job is boring.
Jane's job is boring, so Jane is bored. (not Jane is boring)

If a person is boring, this means that they make other people bored:

Paul always talks about the same things. He's really boring. (= he makes others bored)

Part B: Parallel Pairs of -ing and -ed Adjectives

-ing Adjectives (Tells about the thing/cause)

My job is boring / interesting / tiring / satisfying / depressing.

Julia thinks politics is interesting.
Did you meet anyone interesting at the party?
It was surprising that he passed the exam.
The movie was disappointing.
The news was shocking.

-ed Adjectives (Tells how somebody feels)

I'm bored / interested / tired / satisfied / depressed with my job.

Julia is interested in politics. (not interesting in politics)
Are you interested in buying a car?
Everybody was surprised that he passed the exam.
We were disappointed with the movie.
I was shocked when I heard the news.
