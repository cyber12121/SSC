Unit 88 • Pronouns and determiners

all / all of, most / most of, no / none of etc.quantifiers with and without of: all, some, any, most, much, many, (a) little, (a) few, no/none

We use quantifiers directly before general nouns, but insert 'of' before determiners (the, my, this) and object pronouns (it, us, them).

Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

  • Use quantifiers directly with general nouns without 'of': all cars, some people, most problems.
  • Use quantifier + of before determiners: some of the people, most of my time.
  • Recognize that all and half can be used with or without 'of' before determiners: all (of) my friends, half (of) the money.
  • Always include of before personal pronouns: all of us, some of them, half of it.
  • Use quantifiers independently without a noun: Some cars have four doors and some have two.

Part A — Theory

Part A: Quantifiers with General Nouns (No 'of')

You can use these words directly with a noun (some food / few books etc.):

all • some • any • most • much • many • (a) little • (a) few • no
All cars have wheels.
Some cars can go faster than others.
Many people drive too fast.
I go away most weekends.
I feel really tired. I've got no energy.

Do not say 'all of cars', 'some of people':

Some people learn more easily than others. (not Some of people)

Part B: Quantifier + of + Determiner (Specific Items)

You can use these words with of before the, my, this, these, that, those:

some of • most of • none of etc. + the … / my … / this … / those …
Some of the people I work with are very strange. (not some of people)
None of this money is mine.
Have you read any of these books?
I spent most of the day in bed.

Special Rule: You don't need of after all or half:

All my friends live near here.  or  All of my friends …
Half this money is mine.  or  Half of this money …
general (no 'of')

All flowers are beautiful.
Most problems have a solution.

specific (+ 'of' + determiner)

All (of) these flowers are beautiful.
We solved most of the problems we had.

Part C: Quantifier + of + Pronoun (it / us / you / them)

You can use all of / some of / none of etc. + it / us / you / them. You must use of:

A: Do you like this music?
B: Some of it. Not all of it.

A: How many of these people do you know?
B: None of them. / A few of them.

Do any of you want to come to a party tonight? (said to more than two people)
All of us were late. (not all us)
I've only read half of it. (not half it)

Part D: Quantifiers Used Alone (Without a Noun)

We also use some / most / half alone when the noun is understood:

Some cars have four doors and some have two.
A few of the shops were open, but most were closed.
Half this money is mine, and half is yours. (not the half)
