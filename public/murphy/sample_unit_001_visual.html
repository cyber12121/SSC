Part A — Visual Theory & Scenarios

"I'm driving to work! 🚗"

Study this situation:

Sarah is in her car. She is on her way to work.
She's driving to work.

This means: she is driving now, at the time of speaking. The action is not finished.

💡 Formula: am / is / are + -ing (Present Continuous)

Action in progress, but not at the exact moment of speaking:

Steve is talking to a friend on the phone. He says:
"I’m reading a really good book at the moment. It’s about a man who…"

Steve is on the phone (he is not holding the book open right now). But he has started reading it and hasn't finished it. He is in the middle of reading it!

Changes that have started to happen:

We often use the present continuous with verbs describing changes or trends:

gettingbecoming changingimproving startingbeginning increasingrising fallinggrowing

• The population of the world is increasing very fast. (not increases)

• Is your English getting better? (not Does your English get better)
